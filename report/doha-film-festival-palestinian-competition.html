Four Palestinian films make Doha Film Festival's 15-title competition

The Doha Film Festival, running 19–27 November, has picked four Palestinian films for its international feature competition, including Thirteen in Gaza and Muayad Alayan's Conversation With the Sea. Concrete Land, about a Palestinian Bedouin family in Amman, also competes.

The Doha Film Institute named 15 films from four continents for the festival's international feature competition, which runs 19–27 November (Türkiye Today, 12 Oct; Gulf Times, 7 Oct).

The Palestinian titles are Hosam Abu Dan's Thirteen in Gaza, shot in Gaza; Rabab Khamis's documentary Sama, about a 10-year-old girl in Deir al-Balah who collects plastic and cardboard to sell as fuel, which will have its world premiere there; Muayad Alayan's Conversation With the Sea, set in Jerusalem; and Mohanad Yaqubi's Revolutionaries Never Die, built from 113 film reels kept by Lebanese director Jocelyne Saab, which won a special jury prize at Locarno (Türkiye Today, 12 Oct).

Asmahan Bkerat's Concrete Land is also in the line-up (Türkiye Today, 12 Oct).

Festival director Fatma Hassan Al Remaihi said the event backs filmmakers who challenge established narratives (Türkiye Today, 12 Oct).

Reported from Doha Film Institute, via Türkiye Today; Gulf Times. Filed 12 Oct 2026.
